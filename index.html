Me as an Artist

A few honest words

My name is Rozálie, and I keep myself occupied with music as a pianist, teacher, performer, accompanist, and chamber musician. Despite my Czech origin, I have spent a while in the Netherlands — specifically in the cozy city of Maastricht, where I have been living and studying for five years. During this time, I gained an endless amount of experience, not only as a musician but also as a person.

Music is a big passion, which brings a big responsibility. As a teacher, I help my students to deal with it. During the past few years of teaching, I realized how important it is to encourage pupils' imaginations and allow them to fully appreciate the unique connection between music and fantasy. Creating music and life that are truly meaningful is a core value of my lessons, performances, and consultations.

Studying abroad is also a meaningful step forward. Although it brings uncertainty and discomfort, the outcomes are well worth it. I am here to support those brave enough to follow their purpose and prepare them to manage their Dutch-study experience with ease. At my online consultation, we will discuss Dutch administration, accommodation searches, financial resources, job offers, language questions, and daily life.

I look forward to working with you, whatever brings you here!

My Work in a Nutshell

Pianist

Czech solo pianist Rozálie Stoklasová is known for her distinctive touch, technical skills, and colorful expressivity. She received the IAFM Köln Award in 2024 and made her solo debut at Muziekgebouw Eindhoven in April 2026. Her work also includes chamber music and accompaniment.

Piano Teacher

Rozálie has always been drawn to the art of giving an inspiring, well-structured piano lesson. Her Advanced Teaching Skills training led to a Teaching Certificate and covered psychology, didactics, and piano pedagogy. She teaches private and conservatory students.

Dutch Study Adviser

Rozálie is pursuing a master's degree in classical piano at Conservatorium Maastricht with Dr. Katia Veekmans. She completed her bachelor's degree with a specialization in piano pedagogy in 2026. Her experience of living in the Netherlands informs her study consultations.
