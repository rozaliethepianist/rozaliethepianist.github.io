Privacy Policy

This notice explains the current website and how to contact Rozálie about personal information. Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Who to contact

Rozálie Stoklasová, pianist, piano teacher and Dutch study adviser based in the Netherlands, is the contact for inquiries sent directly to her. Email rozalie.stoklasova@gmail.com about this notice or your personal information.

Browsing this website

The website serves static pages, images and locally hosted fonts. Its own code does not add analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage for marketing. The hosting service may process technical information such as your IP address, requested pages, device information and access times to deliver and secure the site. Host-managed authentication, security logs and cookies are separate from the website code. The private inspection version uses ChatGPT sign-in; OpenAI’s privacy policy applies to that service.

Contact forms and email

The displayed forms cannot submit messages. Their fields remain in the page while you use it; the website code does not transmit or save those entries. Please do not enter sensitive information. Selecting the email link opens your email application. If you choose to email Rozálie, your email address, message and any attachments are processed through the email services used for that correspondence.

Purpose and legal grounds

Information you send directly is used to answer your inquiry and, where requested, discuss or arrange lessons, consultations or performances. Processing needed to take steps at your request or provide an agreed service is based on that purpose. Relevant legal obligations may require some service records. Security and necessary correspondence may involve legitimate interests, subject to your rights. Where consent is required for an optional use, it must be requested separately and can be withdrawn for future processing.

Sharing and international services

Hosting and email providers process information as needed to supply their services. Clicking a social, press-kit or photo-collection link takes you to another service, whose privacy practices apply. Home requests current video titles and preview images from YouTube as the page loads. About uses locally hosted preview images. Selecting a performance opens a privacy-enhanced YouTube player; that connects your browser to YouTube and may involve cookies, device information and processing outside your country. You may instead use the direct YouTube link. This website does not operate an online checkout or payment processor.

Keeping information

The website has no database of form submissions. Email correspondence and service records have separate retention needs: responding to your request, providing an agreed service, resolving questions and meeting applicable legal record-keeping requirements. Contact Rozálie to ask which records and retention periods apply to your situation. The supplied website materials do not specify the host’s log retention or an email retention schedule.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your information, object to relevant processing, or withdraw consent. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and legal obligations. Send requests to the email address above; identity may need to be checked before information is disclosed. You can also complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children and changes

A parent or guardian should handle arrangements for a child’s lessons and avoid sending unnecessary information about the child. This notice should be updated if the website introduces working forms, analytics, new service providers or other processing. Provider-specific retention, transfer safeguards and business contact details must be confirmed for the final public hosting setup.
