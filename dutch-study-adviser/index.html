My story

My name is Rozálie and I keep myself occupied with music as a pianist, teacher, performer, accompanist, and chamber musician. Despite my Czech origin, I have spent a while in the Netherlands, specifically in the cozy city of Maastricht, where I have been living and studying for five years at Conservatorium Maastricht. During this time, I gained a wealth of experience, not only as a musician but also as a person.

Once I decided to continue my music studies in the Netherlands, I remember how demanding it was to start taking steps forward and navigate the information that was available. To get a general overview, I had to spend hours and hours reading articles, stressing out that I missed something important. And even though I was trying really hard, so many mistakes and surprises appeared on the way. I was struggling to find accommodation or part-time job, getting through the Dutch administration, and it actually distracted me from my studies or social life. Therefore, I want to share my experiences with others and help them find their direction in a rather peaceful way.

Studying abroad is a meaningful step forward. Although it brings uncertainty and discomfort, the outcomes are well worth it many times over. I am here to support those who are brave enough to follow their purpose and prepare them to manage their Dutch-study experience with ease. At my online consultation, we will dive into Dutch administration, accommodation searches, financial resources, job offers, language questions, and daily life.

Having completed my bachelor's degree in classical piano, I can see how I learned, grew, experienced, and gained. It is worth so much that I decided to stay for a master's as well. It is a step that is worth it.
